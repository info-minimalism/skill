# Info-Minimalist badge

![Info-Minimalist](info-minimalist-badge-color.svg)

The badge represents your commitment to checking every new in-scope final post before publication with the latest published Info-Minimalism Standard and resolving its supported findings.

Publishers may freely display the unchanged badge when they adopt and maintain this commitment. No fee or individual permission is required. Use the supplied variants and resize proportionally; link to your declaration as described below. This permission covers the badge artwork only, not a license for the Standard or skill.

## Display

- Link the badge to your [publisher declaration](../../references/publisher-self-certification.md#declaration-template), identifying the source and adoption date, with an ordinary route to supporting evaluations and feedback. If a profile does not allow image links, put the declaration link in the bio.
- Prefer at least 240 px wide for the full badge, or 32 × 32 px for the mark. Use the supplied proportions and keep the built-in surrounding space.
- Use color or black on light backgrounds, and white on dark backgrounds. Do not stretch, crop, add effects, or replace the wording with “safe” or “verified.”

The short label is **Info-Minimalist**. The declaration carries the explanation: this is publisher self-certification, not a guarantee of truth or safety. A clean sample cannot replace the continuing checks. Follow the [existing procedure](../../references/publisher-self-certification.md); no registry or outside approval is required.

## Add it to your website

Download the SVG to your site, then adjust these two paths:

```html
<a href="/info-minimalism">
  <img src="/assets/info-minimalist-badge-color.svg"
       width="240" alt="Info-Minimalist — our publishing commitment">
</a>
```

Use your declaration page as the link destination. For an article explaining the concept, identify the badge as an illustration rather than claiming the publication follows the commitment.
