# Calibration Method

**Applies to:** Info-Minimalism Standard v1.0.0

## Objective

Test whether independent evaluators can identify the same payload, preserve it in a minimal adequate rendering, distinguish removable burden from necessary cost, and report comparable diagnostic metrics under the same declared context.

## Required corpus contrasts

The controlled corpus must include paired or near-paired cases for:

- over-specified versus necessary entity references;
- paraphrase repetition versus pedagogically functional repetition;
- delayed payload versus functional narrative ordering;
- qualification versus removable hedging;
- artificial versus necessary fragmentation;
- duplicate channel output versus genuine updates;
- isolated-feed versus in-sequence encounter assumptions;
- bloated content versus terse but under-contextualized content.

Use synthetic fixtures first so the expected transformation property can be designed without copyright or live-context drift. Public social examples may follow only with frozen captures and privacy review.

## Blind protocol

1. Freeze fixture, context card, standard version, cost method, prompt, output template, and evaluator independence statement.
2. Hash the frozen inputs.
3. Obtain at least three independent machine evaluations without access to one another's outputs.
4. Obtain human review of disputed payload preservation and necessity judgments; do not ask humans merely to choose a preferred number.
5. Compare payload maps, MAR fidelity, burden categories, CRAC spans, ARC, and metric ranges.
6. Classify disagreements as boundary, context, payload, preservation, category, measurement, or procedure failures.
7. Revise the method only when a change expresses a general rule rather than a fixture-specific answer.
8. Re-run affected cases plus brevity and necessary-complexity regressions.

## Release review criteria

A proposed method revision is ready for author review when:

- evaluators preserve all designed material payload in controlled cases;
- no deliberately under-contextualized case receives an unqualified favorable efficiency interpretation;
- referent and repetition pairs are distinguished in the intended direction with inspectable reasons;
- sequence and channel burdens are not erased by narrowing the boundary;
- disagreements are expressible through context alternatives or confidence rather than hidden in one score;
- machine-readable records validate; content outcomes and publisher declarations are clearly separated, with no unsupported live certification claim.
- evaluators do not combine words, seconds, clicks, transitions, or exposures into an unsupported common unit.

Numerical agreement alone is insufficient. A shared but lossy MAR is a calibration failure.

## Publisher workflow contrasts

Include a final edited post, an old sample offered as permission for future posts, a missed pre-publication check, an outdated installed version, inaccessible release information, a supported community objection, and falsely described independent agents. Test honest abstention and entertainment exclusion. Use clearly labeled fixture release metadata, not invented observations of the public repository. Preserve raw outputs before adjudication. Machine agreement does not replace author review of material disagreements or real reader testing.
